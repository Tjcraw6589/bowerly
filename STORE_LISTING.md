**Description** (4000 max)
```
Bowerly keeps every bill and subscription in one place: rent or mortgage, electricity, water, phone, internet, insurance, loans, credit cards, and all the streaming and app subscriptions in between. See what goes out each month, mark bills paid as you pay them, and get a reminder before anything is due. It all works on your phone: no account, no bank connection, no cloud.

WHY PEOPLE CHOOSE BOWERLY
• Every recurring payment, not just subscriptions. Bills and subscriptions sit side by side, with separate totals for each.
• Never miss a due date. Bills you pay by hand can be marked paid; anything past its date shows up as overdue, with a nudge the morning after.
• It never asks for your bank login. You type in what you pay and Bowerly does the maths.
• Pro is a one-time purchase. A bill tracker should not be another monthly bill.
• Works offline, anywhere. Your data stays on your device unless you export it.

WHAT YOU GET
• Monthly and yearly totals, split into bills and subscriptions, even when things are paid weekly, quarterly, yearly or on a custom schedule.
• Autopay or pay-by-hand for each bill, and a "~" typical amount for bills that change, like power or water.
• Mark paid with one tap from the bill or straight from the Upcoming list, with undo.
• Reminders on the day, or 1, 3 or 7 days before, at the time you choose. Free-trial reminders before a trial turns into a charge.
• Upcoming view: overdue bills first, then everything due in the next 7, 30 or 90 days with a total.
• Insights: bills vs subscriptions, overdue, trial and paused counts, and your biggest costs.
• Quick add with presets for common bills (rent, utilities, insurance, loans, phone) and popular services, or anything custom with its own emoji and colour.
• Filter by bills or subscriptions and by category: Housing, Utilities, Phone & Internet, Insurance, Loans & Credit, Transport, Taxes & Fees, Streaming and more.
• Pause, resume, cancel or delete.
• Light and dark mode.
• Any currency. Payments in other currencies are shown separately; Bowerly never guesses exchange rates.

BOWERLY PRO (ONE-TIME PURCHASE)
• No ads.
• Unlimited bills and subscriptions (free tracks up to 10 active).
• Spend by category and a 12-month projection of what will leave your account.
• Custom categories.
• CSV export and import.
• Restore on any device signed in to the same App Store account.

PRIVACY
Bowerly has no servers and no accounts. Your bills and subscriptions never leave your phone. The free version shows a small banner advert through Google AdMob; buying Pro removes it completely. Full policy inside the app and at the privacy policy link.

Bowerly is a tracker: it does not pay bills or move money. "Paid" and "overdue" reflect what you mark in the app.

Made by one independent developer. Questions and ideas: tyjcraw@gmail.com
```

## ASO notes

- The name is short and unique; the subtitle carries the two terms people search for ("bills", "subscriptions"), and the keyword field avoids repeating them (Apple already indexes the name and subtitle).
- "Bills", "mark paid", "overdue", "offline" and "one-time" are the differentiators; they appear in the subtitle, promotional text and the first lines of the description, which is what users see before "more".
- Reply to every review in the first weeks; recent ratings carry a lot of weight.
