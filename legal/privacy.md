# Bowerly Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Bowerly is a bill and subscription tracker built to keep your information on your own device. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with data.

## The short version

- Everything you enter into Bowerly (bill and subscription names, amounts, due dates, paid status, notes, categories, settings) is stored only on your device.
- Bowerly has no accounts, no sign-in, no cloud sync and no servers of its own. We cannot see your data.
- The free version shows a small banner advert served by Google AdMob. Advertising is the only part of the app that involves a third party receiving device information, and it is removed entirely by the one-time Pro purchase.
- Pro purchases are processed by Apple or Google and verified through RevenueCat. No payment card details ever reach Bowerly.

## Data you enter

Bill and subscription details, categories, notes and preferences are saved in the app's private storage on your device. They are never transmitted by Bowerly. Deleting the app, or using "Delete all data" in Settings, removes them. If you use the CSV export, the exported file goes wherever you choose to share or save it, and that is under your control.

## Reminders

Renewal and trial reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device by the operating system. No notification content is sent to any server.

## Advertising (free version only)

The free version displays banner adverts using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). To serve and measure adverts, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device model and operating system, and interactions with adverts. How Google uses this information is described in Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy and in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

- In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you are asked for consent through Google's consent form before personalised adverts are requested. You can change your choice at any time by reinstalling the app or through the system advertising settings.
- On iOS, the App Tracking Transparency prompt lets you allow or refuse tracking. If you refuse, adverts are still shown but are not personalised using your identifier.
- You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings at any time.

Purchasing Bowerly Pro removes the Mobile Ads SDK from use; no advert requests are made once Pro is active.

## Purchases

Bowerly Pro is a one-time in-app purchase billed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. To unlock Pro and to restore it on another device, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives the store's purchase receipt and an anonymous, randomly generated app user identifier. RevenueCat does not receive your name, email address or any bill or subscription data you entered. RevenueCat's privacy policy is at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

## Analytics and crash reporting

Bowerly does not include analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. The developer receives only the aggregated, anonymous statistics that Apple and Google provide to every app publisher.

## Children

Bowerly is a general-audience personal finance utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Your rights

Because your data stays on your device, you control it directly: view, edit, export or delete it in the app at any time. For questions about advertising data held by Google or purchase records held by Apple, Google or RevenueCat, please contact those companies directly using the links above. For anything about Bowerly itself, contact the developer at tyjcraw@gmail.com.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address and the "Last updated" date will change. Material changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
